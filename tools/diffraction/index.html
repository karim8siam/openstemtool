Interference & Diffraction – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Plot ideal double-slit interference, single-slit diffraction, or a combined envelope in the far field.

Interactive & 100% Free: Enter your values directly in the calculator above or change the default inputs below to compute outputs instantly in your browser. All computations run locally for immediate privacy and high performance.

What this tool calculates

Explore optical intensity patterns. This computational model is built for university students, educators, lab researchers, and practicing engineers who need transparent, verified scientific calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

Fraunhofer approximation: β=πay/(λD), α=πdy/(λD), I/I₀=(sinβ/β)² cos²α in combined mode.

The calculation evaluates relationships between input variables according to standard scientific principles. Inspect the editable parameter table below to test specific values.

Assumptions and interpretation

  • Small-angle and far-field approximations used. Finite source width, coherence and optical imperfections are neglected.

Educational calculations are not substitutes for professional engineering design approvals, certified laboratory assays, clinical diagnostics, or peer-reviewed scientific publications. Always inspect units, verify physical domains, and evaluate boundary conditions.

The calculator computes results locally in your browser using the established mathematical model: Fraunhofer approximation: β=πay/(λD), α=πdy/(λD), I/I₀=(sinβ/β)² cos²α in combined mode.
