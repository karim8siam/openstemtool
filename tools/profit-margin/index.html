Gross & Net Profit Margin – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Calculate gross margin and net profit margin from revenue and expenses.

Interactive and free: change the example inputs, calculate results in your browser, and export the numerical result or a graph when available. This application requires JavaScript.

What this tool calculates

Calculate gross margin and net profit margin from revenue and expenses.. The calculation is performed locally with the model documented below. Use it to explore relationships between scientific quantities and to check your own classroom calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

Gross margin = (revenue−COGS)/revenue; simple net margin additionally deducts listed other expenses.
