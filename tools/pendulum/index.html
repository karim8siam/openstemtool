Pendulum & Oscillation Analyzer – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Evaluate ideal small-angle motion, period-vs-length curves, and estimate g from optional experimental data.

Interactive and free: change the example inputs, calculate results in your browser, and export the numerical result or a graph when available. This application requires JavaScript.

What this tool calculates

Model pendulum periods and extract gravity. The calculation is performed locally with the model documented below. Use it to explore relationships between scientific quantities and to check your own classroom calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

Simple pendulum approximation T = 2π√(L/g). Experimental g derived from linear fit of T² against L.

Assumptions and interpretation

  • Small-angle approximation becomes inaccurate for large amplitudes. Model excludes air resistance, pivot friction, and string mass.
  • Fitted slope uncertainty and systematics should be reviewed before citing experimental g.

Educational calculations are not substitutes for professional verification, clinical assessment, engineering design approval, or published scientific quality assurance. Always inspect units, domains and model suitability.
