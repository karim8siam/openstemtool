3D Surface Grapher – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Plot a multivariable function z=f(x,y) as a rotatable-style projected wireframe, including numerical sample values.

Interactive and free: change the example inputs, calculate results in your browser, and export the numerical result or a graph when available. This application requires JavaScript.

What this tool calculates

Explore multivariable functions. The calculation is performed locally with the model documented below. Use it to explore relationships between scientific quantities and to check your own classroom calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

29×29 sampled, isometric-style projection of z = sin(sqrt(x^2+y^2)).

Assumptions and interpretation

  • Wireframe is a sampled approximation; it may miss sharp features between grid points.
  • Display projection is not a substitute for exact 3D geometry.

Educational calculations are not substitutes for professional verification, clinical assessment, engineering design approval, or published scientific quality assurance. Always inspect units, domains and model suitability.
