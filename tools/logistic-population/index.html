Logistic Population Growth – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Model constrained population growth with carrying capacity.

Interactive and free: change the example inputs, calculate results in your browser, and export the numerical result or a graph when available. This application requires JavaScript.

What this tool calculates

Model constrained population growth with carrying capacity.. The calculation is performed locally with the model documented below. Use it to explore relationships between scientific quantities and to check your own classroom calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

N(t)=K/[1+(K/N₀−1)e^(−rt)]. Fixed K and r; no migration or stochastic effects.

Assumptions and interpretation

  • An idealized educational model; check its assumptions before interpreting real-world measurements.
