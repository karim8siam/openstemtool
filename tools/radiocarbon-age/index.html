Radiocarbon Age (Simplified) – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Estimate radiocarbon age from measured fraction of original carbon-14.

Interactive and free: change the example inputs, calculate results in your browser, and export the numerical result or a graph when available. This application requires JavaScript.

What this tool calculates

Estimate radiocarbon age from measured fraction of original carbon-14.. The calculation is performed locally with the model documented below. Use it to explore relationships between scientific quantities and to check your own classroom calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

Age=−(half-life/ln2) ln(remaining fraction). Not a calibrated radiocarbon age; real dating requires correction and calibration.
