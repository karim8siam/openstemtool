Drug Concentration Half-Life Model – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Illustrate first-order pharmacokinetic concentration decline (not dosing advice).

Interactive and free: change the example inputs, calculate results in your browser, and export the numerical result or a graph when available. This application requires JavaScript.

What this tool calculates

Illustrate first-order pharmacokinetic concentration decline (not dosing advice).. The calculation is performed locally with the model documented below. Use it to explore relationships between scientific quantities and to check your own classroom calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

C(t)=C₀×2^(−t/t½). One-compartment first-order illustrative model only.

Assumptions and interpretation

  • Not suitable for medication scheduling, prescribing, dose changes, or individual patient predictions.
  • Real elimination can depend on organ function, saturation, metabolism, interactions and dosing history.

Educational calculations are not substitutes for professional verification, clinical assessment, engineering design approval, or published scientific quality assurance. Always inspect units, domains and model suitability.
