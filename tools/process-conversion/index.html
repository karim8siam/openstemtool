Single-Pass Reactor Conversion – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Calculate reactant fractional conversion from inlet and outlet molar flow.

Interactive and free: change the example inputs, calculate results in your browser, and export the numerical result or a graph when available. This application requires JavaScript.

What this tool calculates

Calculate reactant fractional conversion from inlet and outlet molar flow.. The calculation is performed locally with the model documented below. Use it to explore relationships between scientific quantities and to check your own classroom calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

X=(F_A,in−F_A,out)/F_A,in for steady-state reactant flow measurement.
