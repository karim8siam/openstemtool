Scientific Graph Studio – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Create scatter and line plots, apply axis labels and units, compare measurement series, and export your visualization.

Interactive and free: change the example inputs, calculate results in your browser, and export the numerical result or a graph when available. This application requires JavaScript.

What this tool calculates

Create publication-friendly charts from measurements. The calculation is performed locally with the model documented below. Use it to explore relationships between scientific quantities and to check your own classroom calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

Raw data visualization; no regression assumptions are applied.

Assumptions and interpretation

  • Graphs are not evidence of causation. Check axis units and measurement provenance.
  • Error bars represent the user-entered Y standard uncertainties; they are not confidence intervals.

Educational calculations are not substitutes for professional verification, clinical assessment, engineering design approval, or published scientific quality assurance. Always inspect units, domains and model suitability.
