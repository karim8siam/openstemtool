What this tool calculates

Calculate triangle area from three side lengths.. This computational model is built for university students, educators, lab researchers, and practicing engineers who need transparent, verified scientific calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

Heron’s formula A = √(s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)), where s = (a+b+c)/2.
