Z-Score Calculator – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Standardize a measurement using population mean and standard deviation.

Interactive and free: change the example inputs, calculate results in your browser, and export the numerical result or a graph when available. This application requires JavaScript.

What this tool calculates

Standardize a measurement using population mean and standard deviation.. The calculation is performed locally with the model documented below. Use it to explore relationships between scientific quantities and to check your own classroom calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

z = (x−μ)/σ; probability is standard normal CDF Φ(z).
