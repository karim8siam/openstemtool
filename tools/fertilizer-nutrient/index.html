Fertilizer Nutrient Mass – Free Online Scientific Calculator

Calculate supplied nutrient mass from fertilizer mass and concentration.

Interactive and free: change the example inputs, calculate results in your browser, and export the numerical result or a graph when available. This application requires JavaScript.

What this tool calculates

Calculate supplied nutrient mass from fertilizer mass and concentration.. The calculation is performed locally with the model documented below. Use it to explore relationships between scientific quantities and to check your own classroom calculations.

Calculation method and underlying formula

Nutrient mass = fertilizer mass × labeled mass fraction. Follow agronomic guidance and local regulations for real application.
